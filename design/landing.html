The thesis

The labor shortage isn't a matching problem.
It's a supply problem.

Every staffing platform, job board, and recruiter is competing to allocate a pool of skilled trades that is structurally too small. You can win that auction and still not have enough plumbers. Foundry doesn't compete for the pool — it grows the pool.

Everyone else Allocation

Move existing workers around.
  • — Bid against every other firm for the same credentialed trades
  • — Zero net-new supply enters the market
  • — Costs rise as scarcity rises; no compounding
  • — Humans manually run the funnel, every single time

Foundry Manufacture

Convert career-switchers into the trade.
  • → Targets people not yet in the trade and pulls them in
  • → Each cycle adds credentialed workers to the market
  • → The model learns your pipeline; every loop gets cheaper
  • → Agents run the funnel autonomously, end to end
The mechanism

A closed, compounding loop of six agents.

Demand is detected, supply is manufactured, workers are dispatched — and the outcomes flow back into the model that started it. Nothing here is a dashboard waiting for a human. It runs.

The fleet

Six agents. One handoff chain.

Each agent owns one job, emits a typed artifact, and hands off to the next. You can read every artifact — no black boxes.
